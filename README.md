# 5.8 GHz WPT Microstrip Patch Antenna

## Electromagnetic Investigation and Design Optimization of a 5.8 GHz Microstrip Patch Antenna with Defected Ground Structure for Wireless Power Transfer Applications

> 🚧 **Project Status: Ongoing**

## 📡 Overview

This repository contains the design, simulation, optimization, fabrication, and experimental investigation of a **5.8 GHz microstrip patch antenna** for **Wireless Power Transfer (WPT)** applications.

The project investigates the electromagnetic performance of a conventional microstrip patch antenna and studies the effects of **Defected Ground Structures (DGS)** and geometric modifications on antenna characteristics and wireless power transfer performance.

The work follows a systematic design approach, beginning with a conventional rectangular microstrip patch antenna and progressing through design modifications and optimization.

---

## 🎯 Objectives

- Design a rectangular microstrip patch antenna operating at the **5.8 GHz ISM band**.
- Investigate the effect of **Defected Ground Structures (DGS)** on antenna performance.
- Study the influence of antenna geometry modifications on electromagnetic characteristics.
- Analyze important antenna parameters, including:
  - Return Loss ($S_{11}$)
  - VSWR
  - Gain
  - Bandwidth
  - Radiation Pattern
  - Surface Current Distribution
  - Axial Ratio
- Optimize the antenna design for **Wireless Power Transfer** applications.
- Perform a two-antenna WPT analysis using the transmission coefficient ($S_{21}$).
- Fabricate and experimentally validate the final antenna design.

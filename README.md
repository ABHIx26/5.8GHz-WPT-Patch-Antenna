# 5.8 GHz WPT Microstrip Patch Antenna

## Electromagnetic Investigation and Design Optimization of a 5.8 GHz Microstrip Patch Antenna with Defected Ground Structure for Wireless Power Transfer Applications

> **Project Status: Ongoing**

---

## 📡 Overview

This repository documents the design, simulation, optimization, fabrication, and experimental investigation of a **5.8 GHz microstrip patch antenna** for **Wireless Power Transfer (WPT)** applications.

The project focuses on investigating the electromagnetic performance of a conventional microstrip patch antenna and studying the effect of **Defected Ground Structures (DGS)** and other geometric modifications on antenna characteristics.

The work follows a systematic design evolution, beginning with a conventional rectangular microstrip patch antenna and progressing through structural modifications and optimization for improved performance in wireless power transfer applications.

## 🎯 Objectives

The main objectives of this project are:

- To design a conventional rectangular microstrip patch antenna operating at the **5.8 GHz ISM band**.
- To investigate the effect of **Defected Ground Structures (DGS)** on antenna performance.
- To study the influence of antenna geometry modifications on electromagnetic characteristics.
- To analyze important antenna parameters, including:
  - Return Loss ($S_{11}$)
  - VSWR
  - Gain
  - Bandwidth
  - Radiation Pattern
  - Surface Current Distribution
  - Axial Ratio
- To optimize the antenna design for **Wireless Power Transfer (WPT)** applications.
- To investigate wireless power transfer performance using a two-antenna configuration and the transmission coefficient ($S_{21}$).
- To fabricate and experimentally validate the final antenna design using appropriate measurement equipment.

## 🔮 Future Work

The current work provides a foundation for further investigation and development in microwave antenna-based wireless power transfer systems.

Possible future improvements include:

- Further optimization of the antenna geometry for improved impedance matching and radiation performance.
- Investigation of alternative Defected Ground Structure (DGS) geometries.
- Improvement of circular polarization characteristics and axial ratio performance.
- Investigation of alternative substrate materials with lower dielectric losses.
- Experimental analysis of wireless power transfer performance at different antenna separation distances.
- Investigation of antenna alignment and polarization effects on power transfer.
- Integration of a rectifier circuit to develop a complete **rectenna system**.
- Improvement of RF-to-DC conversion efficiency.
- Development of antenna arrays for improved gain and wireless power transfer capability.
- Investigation of practical applications in low-power wireless charging and energy harvesting systems.
